How a project runs

Typical identity project, 10 weeks
  1. W1

    Workshop

    Half a day at your place. We look at the product, the shelf and the people who buy it.

  2. W3

    Two directions

    Two complete routes, shown on real things: labels, a homepage, a van.

  3. W6

    One system

    We build out the chosen route into a working toolkit and test it in print.

  4. W10

    Handover

    Files, guidelines and a call with your printer. We stay on for three months of fixes.
