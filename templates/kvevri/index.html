A year inside a kvevri

How the amber wine in your glass was made. Nothing is added except time.

  1. Sep

    Rtveli, the harvest

    Grapes are picked by hand and pressed with their skins, stems and seeds.

  2. Oct

    Into the jar

    Everything goes into a clay kvevri buried up to its neck in the cellar floor.

  3. Nov

    Sealed underground

    The jar is closed with a stone lid and clay. The earth keeps it at 14 °C.

  4. Apr

    Opened in spring

    The wine is drawn off the skins, now amber, dry and ready to pour.
