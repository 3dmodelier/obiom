Three steps, no packaging

First visit? We lend you jars for free. Return them next time, or keep them for a small deposit.

  1. Weigh

    Put your empty container on the scale at the door. We print a sticker with its weight.

  2. Fill

    Fill it from any of our 140 dispensers, bins and taps. Take as little as you need.

  3. Pay

    We weigh it again at the till and you pay only for what's inside.
