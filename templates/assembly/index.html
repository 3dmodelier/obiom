Assembly

A two-day conference for people who build physical things: hardware, furniture, tools, machines and the factories that make them.

Get a ticket
14–15 May 2027Fenix Hall, Rotterdam · 600 seats
Software eats the world. Someone still has to build the plate.

Assembly brings together engineers, designers and makers who ship real objects. No product launches on stage, no sponsor keynotes. Just people showing how they build, what broke on the way, and what they would do differently.
