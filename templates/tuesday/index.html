Questions

Can students book without an account?

Yes. They enter a name, email and pay. Tuesday creates the profile for them in the background.

Which payment methods work?

Cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay through Stripe. Money arrives in your bank every Monday.

Can I move my students from a spreadsheet?

Upload a CSV with names and emails. Remaining class passes come across too.

What happens after the free trial?

Nothing, unless you choose to continue. We never charge without asking first.
